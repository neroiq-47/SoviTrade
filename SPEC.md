# SoviTrade Product Specification

**Status:** v1 front-end with live market data; no real trading or payments yet.

## Overview
SoviTrade is a non-custodial auto-trading dashboard for tokenised US stocks and ETFs on Solana (xStocks, Ondo, Backpack via Jupiter). Users choose a strategy (Smart DCA, Grid, Trend, Rebalancing), set guardrails the bots can't break, and keep custody in their own Swig smart wallet.
